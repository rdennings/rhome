# Code of Conduct

rhome follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and take feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the project and the people in it. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks aren't tolerated, in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) or anywhere you're representing the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer (@rdennings) privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code of conduct.
